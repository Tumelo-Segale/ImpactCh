About Our Company

Founded on Purpose.

Impact Compass Holdings (PTY) LTD, established as Segale Incorporated and later rebranded, was founded with a singular vision: to create a multifaceted platform that addresses the complex needs of modern development in Africa and beyond.

Under the leadership of Dr. Jacob Segale, we have grown into a dedicated academic publishing house, operating through two specialized divisions: Impact Journals and Impact Publishing.

Our mission is to provide influential publishing and high-impact, peer-reviewed academic journals that foster growth, innovation, and positive societal change.

Global Reach

We connect African perspectives with global academic and professional networks.

Rigorous Quality

From peer-reviewed research to professional publishing, quality is our bedrock.

Social Impact

Every project we undertake is measured by its contribution to societal well-being.
