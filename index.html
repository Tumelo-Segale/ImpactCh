About Our Company

Founded on Purpose.

Impact Compass Holdings (PTY) LTD, established as Segale Incorporated and later rebranded, was founded with a singular vision: to create a multifaceted platform that addresses the complex needs of modern development in Africa and beyond.

Under the leadership of Dr. Jacob Segale, we have expanded from a boutique consultancy into a comprehensive powerhouse of knowledge and trade, operating through four specialized divisions.

Our mission is to provide expert consulting, influential publishing, high-impact academic journals, and quality products that foster growth, innovation, and positive societal change.

Global Reach

We connect African perspectives with global academic and professional networks.

Rigorous Quality

From peer-reviewed research to professional consulting, quality is our bedrock.

Social Impact

Every project we undertake is measured by its contribution to societal well-being.
